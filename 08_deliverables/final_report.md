# Final Project Report — E-commerce Sales Performance

**Prepared for:** Daniel (Simulated Client)  
**Project:** E-commerce Sales Data Cleaning and Analysis  
**Reporting Period:** January–October 2025  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Google Colab, Power BI

> This report belongs to a simulated portfolio project using synthetic data. It does not represent a real paid client engagement.

## 1. Project Summary

The objective of this project was to clean e-commerce sales data, evaluate sales performance, identify key revenue contributors, and present the findings in a business-friendly dashboard.

The project included data quality assessment, cleaning, revenue validation, KPI development, exploratory analysis, and dashboard creation.

## 3. Data Preparation Results

The original dataset contained 304 records.

The cleaning process removed four exact duplicate rows, leaving 300 records in the deduplicated working dataset.

Missing and invalid values were identified and documented. Two completed orders were excluded from revenue analysis because of invalid numerical data.

The final revenue analysis included 185 valid completed orders.

## 4. Main Business Findings

1. **Top Product:** 27-inch Monitor — £14,705.85.
2. **Top Region:** West — £13,751.56.
3. **Top Category:** Electronics — £20,812.88.
4. **Top Sales Channel:** Social Media — £15,463.27.
5. **Highest Revenue Month:** July 2025 — £5,341.81.

## 5. Recommendations

- Monitor the availability and performance of the 27-inch Monitor.
- Review the factors contributing to West's regional performance.
- Continue monitoring Electronics category performance.
- Evaluate Social Media campaign effectiveness alongside marketing costs.
- Investigate the September revenue decline and October recovery.
- Improve the recording of Product, Region, and Sales Channel information.

## 6. Data Limitations

The dataset does not include sufficient information to calculate profit, gross margin, or marketing ROI.

The analysis also cannot establish the causes of regional or monthly revenue differences.

Unknown descriptive values were retained where valid revenue could be calculated.
