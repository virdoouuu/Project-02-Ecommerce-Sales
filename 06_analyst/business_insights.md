q# Business Insights — E-commerce Sales Performance

## 1. Executive Summary

The analysis identified £37,207.55 in revenue from 185 valid completed orders, with 648 units sold.

The results show that revenue is concentrated in the Electronics category and the 27-inch Monitor product. The West region and Social Media channel generated the highest revenue among their respective dimensions.

Monthly revenue peaked in July 2025, followed by a decline in September and a recovery in October.

These findings provide a starting point for reviewing product priorities, regional performance, and sales channel strategy.

## 2. Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Product Performance

**Observation**

The 27-inch Monitor generated the highest product revenue:

- Revenue: £14,705.85
- Share of total revenue: approximately 39.5%

**Business Meaning**

A substantial portion of recorded revenue comes from one product. This makes its sales performance important to monitor.

**Recommendation**

Monitor the availability and sales performance of the 27-inch Monitor. Review its stock levels and purchasing patterns to reduce the risk of missed sales opportunities.

**Further Data Needed**

Inventory levels, stock-out records, product costs, and gross margins.

### Finding 2 — Regional Performance

**Observation**

The West region generated the highest regional revenue:

- Revenue: £13,751.56
- Share of total revenue: approximately 37.0%

**Business Meaning**

West is the strongest revenue-generating region in this dataset.

**Recommendation**

Investigate the sales activities, customer demand, and product mix in West. Determine whether successful approaches can be adapted to other regions.

**Further Data Needed**

Customer counts, regional marketing expenditure, order frequency, and regional operating costs.

### Finding 3 — Category Performance

**Observation**

Electronics generated the highest category revenue:

- Revenue: £20,812.88
- Share of total revenue: approximately 55.9%

**Business Meaning**

Electronics represents the largest category contribution to recorded sales revenue.

**Recommendation**

Continue monitoring Electronics performance while evaluating opportunities to grow other categories.

**Further Data Needed**

Category-level costs, profit margins, inventory levels, and customer demand.

### Finding 4 — Sales Channel Performance

**Observation**

Social Media generated the highest sales channel revenue:

- Revenue: £15,463.27
- Share of total revenue: approximately 41.6%

**Business Meaning**

Social Media is the leading channel by recorded revenue.

**Recommendation**

Review campaign performance and associated marketing costs before increasing investment in this channel.

**Further Data Needed**

Advertising spend, campaign attribution, conversion rates, customer acquisition cost, and channel-level profit.

### Finding 5 — Monthly Revenue Trend

**Observation**

- July 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue at £5,341.81.
- August remained strong at £5,269.78.
- September declined to £3,065.22.
- October recovered to £4,672.85.

**Business Meaning**

Monthly revenue varied during the January–October 2025 period. September's decline and October's recovery deserve further investigation.

**Recommendation**

Review marketing activities, product availability, promotions, and order patterns during these months to identify possible contributing factors.

**Further Data Needed**

Campaign calendars, stock records, pricing changes, and historical sales data from additional years.

## 3. Data Quality Considerations

Some revenue was associated with missing descriptive information:

| Dimension | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Unknown Product | £438.00 |
| Unknown Region | £61.16 |
| Unknown Sales Channel | £85.46 |

These transactions were retained in the revenue analysis where the numerical fields and order status were valid.

The Unknown labels indicate incomplete descriptive information, not zero revenue.

## 4. Business Recommendations — Priority Summary

| Priority | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| High | Monitor the 27-inch Monitor | Largest individual product revenue |
| High | Review Electronics performance | Largest category contribution |
| Medium | Investigate West region performance | Highest regional revenue |
| Medium | Evaluate Social Media campaigns | Highest channel revenue |
| Medium | Investigate September's decline | Significant monthly revenue decrease |
| Ongoing | Improve missing data capture | Better reporting accuracy |

## 5. Important Limitations

This analysis identifies revenue patterns but does not establish their causes.

Revenue is not the same as profit. The dataset does not contain sufficient cost information to assess profitability or marketing return on investment.

The monthly analysis covers January–October 2025 only. It is not sufficient to establish recurring seasonal patterns.

## 6. Conclusion

The business generated £37,207.55 in valid completed-order revenue during the analyzed period.

The 27-inch Monitor, Electronics category, West region, and Social Media channel were the leading revenue contributors in their respective dimensions.

The recommended next step is to combine sales data with inventory, cost, and marketing data to support deeper business decisions.
